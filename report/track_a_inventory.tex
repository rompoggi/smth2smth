\subsection{Executive summary}
\label{sec:tracka:inventory:summary}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Best public Track-A submission to date}: a Phase-2
   single-stream \texttt{avanced\_resnet50\_tsm} run from scratch under
   \file{configs/experiment/track\_a\_phase2.yaml} with a 150-epoch
   cosine schedule, attentive head, EMA, and multi-scale flip-TTA at
   submit reached \(\mathbf{\approx 42\,\%}\) public Top-1 on an earlier
   run whose checkpoint was lost.  The active rerun
   \file{logs/trackA\_phase2\_randaugT\_attn\_dropPath0.1\_adamw\_ema\_amp\_4frames\_officialVal\_ep150\_20260516.log}
   is at epoch~38/150 with EMA val \(0.3936\) and climbing monotonically;
   see \S\ref{sec:tracka:inventory:phase2-150ep}.  This recipe supersedes
   the dual-stream Run~A1b (EMA val \(39.47\,\%\), public \(37.69\,\%\))
   as the leading single-model path.
\item \textbf{Best on-disk honest val from the VideoMAE SSL sweep}:
   \emph{piranha} \(\mathbf{35.60\,\%}\) EMA (40 FT epochs, ep~32), then
   \emph{rouget} \(34.96\,\%\) (Phase~2a honest), \emph{raie}
   \(34.80\,\%\) (6-head probe), \emph{roussette} \(33.61\,\%\). \textbf{None
   have been submitted.}  Honest val here means
   \cfgkey{dataset.use\_official\_val}\,=\,\code{true} \emph{and} the
   absence of any \cfgkey{dataset.include\_val\_in\_train} override,
   verified from each finetune log.
\item \textbf{Only Kaggle SSL data point}: \emph{rouget} \(36.0\,\%\)
   public from \file{rouget\_ft.pt} \emph{after} Phase~2b
   (leaky: train+val labels both in training).  Aligns with the Phase~2a
   honest val of \(34.96\,\%\), not the inflated \(\sim 60\,\%\) Phase~2b
   internal --- training on validation \emph{did not} beat the dual-stream
   submission (\S\ref{sec:tracka:runlog}).
\item \textbf{Supervised \texttt{video\_mae\_vit} from scratch}: every
   variant (\emph{truite}, \emph{sardine}, \emph{truite AP},
   \emph{rouget}, \emph{roussette}, \emph{raie}) plateaued at
   \(\le 6\,\%\) val on \(\sim 45\,\)k labelled clips
   (\S\ref{sec:tracka:vit-from-scratch-verdict}).  The audit confirms the
   verdict: no further runs in this family are warranted.
\item \textbf{Local checkpoint asymmetry}: only
   \file{checkpoints/track\_a/ssl/rouget\_encoder.pt} and the leaky
   \file{rouget\_ft.pt} are present on this VM.  The piranha / raie /
   roussette / truite encoders and FT weights referenced by
   \file{experiments.md} live on the producing VMs and must be retrieved
   before any submission.  The Run~A1b checkpoint
   \file{dual\_stream\_long\_class\_boost\_lr1\_20260515\_025557.pt} is
   likewise absent from \file{checkpoints/track\_a/}; the current
   \file{best\_model.pt} (\(\sim 1\,\)GiB, dated 15~May 23:37) is
   consistent in size with the dual-stream model but its identity must
   be confirmed prior to any resubmission.
\item \textbf{Multi-scale TTA caveat for VideoMAE}: \file{submit.py}'s
   \code{\_rescale\_video} bilinearly resizes spatial dims, but
   \code{PatchEmbed3D} requires \code{H \% patch\_size == 0}
   (\file{video\_mae.py} line~65).  For \texttt{patch\_size=16} and
   \texttt{image\_size=224}, scales like \(0.875\) (\(\to 196\)) or
   \(1.125\) (\(\to 252\)) yield non-divisible sides and crash the patch
   embedding.  \emph{Multi-scale TTA is therefore not safe with VideoMAE
   ViT} unless restricted to scales producing patch-divisible sides
   (e.g.\ \(192/224 \approx 0.857\), \(256/224 \approx 1.143\)).  Rouget
   was correctly submitted with \cfgkey{training.tta\_scales}\,=\,\code{[1.0]}.
\item \textbf{Top recommendation}: mirror the Phase-2 150-epoch
   single-stream best-EMA checkpoint (file path in
   \S\ref{sec:tracka:inventory:phase2-150ep}, on Thomas's VM under
   \file{/Data/thomas.turkieh/.../checkpoints/track\_a/}) to this VM
   every few epochs so the \(\sim 42\,\%\) recipe survives a host loss
   this time, let the run finish 150 epochs, and submit with
   \cfgkey{training.tta\_scales}\,=\,\code{[0.875,\,1.0,\,1.125]} and
   flip-TTA.  After that, retrieve and submit \emph{piranha} (highest
   SSL honest val) with flip-TTA only; rerun \emph{rouget} Phase~2a;
   complete \emph{truite} Phase~2.  Softmax-average the Phase-2
   single-stream output with \emph{piranha} for the final submission.
\end{itemize}

   the YAMLs.

\subsection{Phase~2 single-stream long-budget rerun --- current best path}

\paragraph{What the run is.}  Preset
\file{configs/experiment/track\_a\_phase2.yaml} on
\texttt{avanced\_resnet50\_tsm} (R50 with TSM channel shift inserted in
every bottleneck's \texttt{conv1}, \texttt{shift\_div=8},
\texttt{shift\_place=blockres}), trained \emph{from scratch}
(\cfgkey{model.init\_from}\,=\,\code{null}, no SSL warm-start), 4
frames, image size 224, official val.  Optimiser AdamW, \texttt{lr}=1e-4,
\texttt{wd}=0.05, cosine schedule with 10-epoch linear warmup and
\texttt{min\_lr}=1e-6 over a 150-epoch budget; early-stopping patience
25.  Attentive head with 4 heads, \texttt{drop\_path\_rate}=0.1,
\texttt{dropout}=0.5, \texttt{label\_smoothing}=0.1,
\texttt{videomix\_mode}=\texttt{frame\_mixup} (\(\alpha=5.0\),
\(p=0.5\)), AMP fp16, EMA decay 0.999 with both live and EMA evaluated
each epoch.  RandAugment-T (\(n=2\), \(m=9\),
\texttt{temporal\_plus}) with \texttt{random\_horizontal\_flip}=\code{true}
and \texttt{random\_crop} padding 32.  At submit time, multi-scale TTA
\texttt{tta\_scales}=\texttt{[0.875, 1.0, 1.125]}, flip with auto
left/right pair remap, no logit-adjust.

\paragraph{Why this recipe wins.}  Ordered by likely impact.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{150-epoch budget with patience 25.}  The dual-stream A1b
   ran the same data stack (frame\_mixup, RandAugment-T, official val,
   AMP, EMA) but early-stopped at epoch~39 with patience~3.  Phase~2
   keeps the cosine running from 1e-4 down to 1e-6 over a full
   150-epoch window, and the EMA accumulates real gain in the
   late-cosine phase: the log shows monotonic climbs of about
   \(+0.6\)\,pp every 3--4 epochs in the 0.30--0.39 band, with no early
   stall.  Truncated at patience~3 this run would have died near
   \(\sim 32\,\%\) like A1 did, not \(\sim 42\,\%\).
\item \textbf{CNN + TSM inductive bias at \(\sim 45\,\)k labelled clips.}
   Confirmed by \S\ref{sec:tracka:vit-from-scratch-verdict}: ViT-B from
   random init plateaus at \(\le 6\,\%\) val.  TSM channel-shift in
   every bottleneck \texttt{conv1} gives temporal context for free with
   no extra parameters.  SSL VideoMAE-ViT recovers part of this
   (piranha \(35.6\,\%\) honest val), but at 4 frames and 45\,k clips a
   long-trained CNN still beats it.
\item \textbf{Multi-scale TTA \texttt{[0.875, 1.0, 1.125]} is safe for
   this CNN but not for the SSL ViT.}  As recorded in
   \S\ref{sec:tracka:inventory:verification}, \texttt{PatchEmbed3D}
   requires \(H \bmod 16 = 0\); \(0.875 \times 224 = 196\) and \(1.125
   \times 224 = 252\) are not patch-divisible, so the SSL fish runs
   must submit with \texttt{[1.0]} only.  A fully-convolutional R50 has
   no such constraint, and this asymmetry alone is typically worth
   \(\sim 0.5\)--\(1.5\,\)pp public Top-1.
\item \textbf{Regularisation stack that actually has time to pay off.}
   \texttt{drop\_path}=0.1 (linearly scaled) \(+\) \texttt{dropout}=0.5
   \(+\) \texttt{label\_smoothing}=0.1 \(+\) frame\_mixup \(\alpha\)=5.0
   \(+\) RandAugment-T \(+\) EMA 0.999 \(+\) AMP all compose
   coherently.  Dual-stream A1b runs the same stack minus
   \texttt{dropout} (it is 0 there) but for only 39 epochs.  SSL piranha
   runs with \texttt{dropout}=0 and 40 FT epochs.  Phase~2 is the only
   candidate that pays the regularisation tax \emph{and} trains long
   enough to claim the credit.
\item \textbf{\texttt{random\_horizontal\_flip}=\code{true} is safe here
   because of the submit-time pair remap.}  The auto-derived 018\,\(\leftrightarrow\)\,019
   permutation
   (\file{src/smth2smth/pipelines/submit.py} lines 213--268) re-aligns
   the flipped softmax for direction-sensitive classes; for the
   majority of classes flipping is label-preserving and acts as a free
   2\(\times\) augmentation.  The SSL ViT runs play conservative
   (\texttt{horizontal\_flip}=\code{false}) and forgo this win.
\item \textbf{Simpler long-tail handling.}  No
   \texttt{class\_balance\_sampler}, no \texttt{class\_balance\_loss},
   no \texttt{class\_boosting}, no time-reversal, no logit-adjust.
   Three of the failed supervised ViT runs (rouget / roussette / raie)
   bolted these on and still plateaued at \(\le 6\,\%\); the dual-stream
   A1b had \texttt{class\_boosting} on but it is hard to credit it for
   the 39.47\,\% honest val given the very tight early-stop.
   \texttt{frame\_mixup} \(+\) \texttt{label\_smoothing} \(+\) cosine
   over 150 epochs gives a softer-but-stronger version of the same
   long-tail correction without an explicit class weight.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Risks specific to this recipe.}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Checkpoint lives only on Thomas's VM
   (\file{/Data/thomas.turkieh/.../*\_best.pt}); a single host loss
   already cost us the \(\sim 42\,\%\) once.  Mirror the
   \texttt{*\_best.pt} to
   \file{/Data/romain.poggi/smth2smth/checkpoints/track\_a/archive/}
   every 10 epochs while the rerun is alive.
\item The auto pair remap is keyed on class folder names containing
   \texttt{from\_left\_to\_right} / \texttt{from\_right\_to\_left}.  If
   the dataset layout drifts, the remap silently becomes identity and
   \texttt{horizontal\_flip} starts producing wrong labels.  Verify the
   \texttt{[tta] flip-pair remap: ...} line at submit time lists at
   least one pair.
\item \texttt{num\_classes} in the log is 33 (the legacy on-disk class
   count); the embedded
   \texttt{trained\_class\_indices} should be checked against the
   actual 50-class submission head before the final submit.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{VideoMAE SSL pretrain.}  Code:
\file{src/smth2smth/pipelines/pretrain\_videomae.py}; tube mask 0.75 by

(\(0.85 \to 0.25\) at 100 ep, \(0.234\) at 200 ep for \emph{rouget}) is

\paragraph{VideoMAE SSL fine-tune via \cfgkey{model.init\_from}.}  Code:
\file{src/smth2smth/pipelines/train.py} lines~428--441 specialises the

\paragraph{Submit pipeline.}  Code:
\file{src/smth2smth/pipelines/submit.py}.  Three relevant routines:
\code{\_build\_untrained\_mask} ([line~186]) sets logits at never-trained
class indices to \(-\infty\); \code{\_build\_flip\_class\_permutation}
([line~213]) auto-derives the 018/019 left/right pair remap;
\code{\_rescale\_video} ([line~292]) bilinearly resizes spatial dims for
multi-scale TTA.  \textbf{Status: OK with one VideoMAE-specific
restriction.}  Pitfalls:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Multi-scale TTA + VideoMAE}: each scaled clip must have
   \(H, W\) divisible by \cfgkey{model.patch\_size}=16.  At
   \texttt{image\_size}=224 the default
   \(\{0.875, 1.0, 1.125\}\) produces \(\{196, 224, 252\}\); 196 and 252
   are not patch-divisible and would crash \texttt{PatchEmbed3D}.  Submit
   VideoMAE ViT runs with \cfgkey{training.tta\_scales}=\texttt{[1.0]}
   (already done for \emph{rouget}).
\item \textbf{Untrained-class mask}: derived from
   \texttt{trained\_class\_indices} embedded in the checkpoint.  When
   the head width or class folder set is updated, regenerate the mask by
   retraining or by clearing the stale list, otherwise valid logits get
   silently zeroed.
\item \textbf{\texttt{model.pretrained} flag}: \texttt{submit.py} reads
   \texttt{saved\_cfg.model.pretrained} to decide on ImageNet normalisation;
   VideoMAE runs save \texttt{pretrained: false} (no normalisation), so
   no action needed for the current submissions.
\end{enumerate}
