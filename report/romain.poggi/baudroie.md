**Comments:**

1. **LR sensitivity (HF pooler):** optimum at **3e-4 ≈ 1e-3** (0.758–0.759) > 1e-4 (0.754) ≫ 5e-3 (0.692). High LR clearly hurts.
2. **MQ probe is more LR-fragile:** Q=16 @ LR 3e-3 (b08) **diverged** (train top1 collapsed to 0.07); keep MQ LR ≤ 1e-3.
3. **Q-ablation (MQ, LR 1e-3):** Q=8 (0.753) ≥ Q=16 (0.744) ≈ Q=32 (0.742) — doubling queries does **not** help; Q=8 marginally best (echoes Track A + BLIP-2).
4. **Pretrained Meta pooler currently beats the from-scratch probes:** b03/b04 (HF, ~0.758) > MQ b07–b10 (≤0.753) > 4-block scratch b11 (0.713). So the multi-query hypothesis (Exp 1) is **not** winning yet — the pooler's pretrained init dominates at ep6–8. (Caveat: mid-training; MQ may close the gap by ep15.)
5. **Headline:** best holdout_clean ~0.759 is **above** the doc's 70.8 % baseline and approaching the 74 % target — *if* clean-holdout ≈ LB (still to confirm by submission). Large train↔holdout gap (0.78–0.94 vs 0.74–0.76) = overfit; not yet converged.
